Modifications
-------------
The FlashSR generator and helpers are packaged under
pymss_core.modules.flashsr. The generator's unused discriminators and
training-related imports were omitted. A FlashSR model-factory wrapper and
sample-rate attributes were added. Upstream checkpoint parameter names are
preserved. Source and license notices were added to the relocated files.
